Conference Overview

Advancing Intelligent Technologies for a Connected Future

ARIIA 2027 (3rd International Conference on Augmented Reality, Intelligent Systems & Industrial Automation), hosted by Hung Vuong University, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, provides a global platform for researchers, academicians, industry leaders, innovators, and policymakers to exchange ideas, present cutting-edge research, and establish international collaborations in emerging intelligent technologies.

Conference Vision

To foster innovation, interdisciplinary research, and international collaboration in Artificial Intelligence, Augmented Reality, Intelligent Systems, Industrial Automation, and Industry 5.0 technologies.

Who Should Attend?

Researchers • Faculty Members • Industry Professionals • Doctoral Scholars • Startups • Technology Innovators • Policy Makers

Venue

Hung Vuong University
Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

ARIIA 2027 will be hosted by Hung Vuong University, one of Vietnam's leading higher education institutions, located in the vibrant city of Ho Chi Minh City. The university offers a modern academic environment, advanced research facilities, and excellent accessibility for international delegates.

The conference is planned to be conducted primarily in offline mode. Additional participation details, including any hybrid or virtual presentation options, will be announced closer to the conference.
