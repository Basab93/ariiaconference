About Ariia 2027

Advancing Intelligent Technologies for a Connected Future

ARIIA 2027 (3rd International Conference on Augmented Reality, Intelligent Systems & Industrial Automation) is a premier international platform organized by World Research Union (WRU) and LCARE Ltd. (UK), and hosted by Hung Vuong University, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

The conference brings together researchers, academicians, industry leaders, innovators, and policymakers from around the world to exchange ideas, present cutting-edge research, foster interdisciplinary collaboration, and promote technological innovations in the fields of Artificial Intelligence, Augmented Reality, Intelligent Systems, Industrial Automation, and emerging digital technologies.

Conference Vision

To foster innovation, interdisciplinary research, and international collaboration in Artificial Intelligence, Augmented Reality, Intelligent Systems, Industrial Automation, and Industry 5.0 technologies.
