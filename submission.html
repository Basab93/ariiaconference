Paper Submission

Submit your original research contributions to ARIIA 2027.

3rd International Conference on Augmented Reality, Intelligent Systems & Industrial Automation

Overview

Academicians, researchers, scientists, industry professionals, and practitioners are invited to submit original, unpublished research papers that are not currently under review by any other conference or journal.

Original Articles

Only original and unpublished manuscripts are accepted.

Peer Review

All submissions undergo rigorous double-blind peer review.

Quality First

Evaluation based on originality, quality and technical contribution.

Presentation

At least one author must register and present the accepted paper.

Plagiarism Policy

ARIIA 2027 has zero tolerance for any intentional breach of copyright law. Authors are strongly encouraged to screen their manuscripts using plagiarism detection software such as Turnitin or iThenticate before submission.

  • The overall manuscript similarity index should be less than 10%.
  • Similarity from any single source must not exceed 3%.
  • Manuscripts will be evaluated individually. A high similarity percentage from a single source will be treated as a serious concern.
  • Common mathematical formulas, equations, technical terms, and standard expressions may be excluded from similarity evaluation where appropriate.
  • All content reproduced from books, journals, conference papers, websites, or other published sources must be properly cited and referenced.
  • Reuse of an author's previously published work requires appropriate permission, citation, and acknowledgement.

Whether intentional or unintentional, plagiarism is considered a serious violation of academic ethics. Plagiarism includes copying ideas, text, data, figures, tables, graphs, or any other intellectual work and presenting it as original without proper attribution.

ARIIA 2027 considers a manuscript plagiarized if it contains 20% or more similarity with proper citation, or 10% or more similarity without appropriate citation.

We accept only original research articles. Any manuscript found to contain plagiarism at any stage of the review or publication process may be rejected, withdrawn, or removed from the conference proceedings.

Important: If a submitted manuscript is found to be plagiarized, it may be rejected immediately. Once an author confirms participation and completes registration, the registration fee will not be refunded in cases involving plagiarism or research misconduct.
